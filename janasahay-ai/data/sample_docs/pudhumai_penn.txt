Pudhumai Penn Scheme (Moovalur Ramamirtham Ammaiyar Higher Education Assurance Scheme) - Tamil Nadu
Sample demo notes - always verify details on the official Tamil Nadu government website.

Benefits: Monthly assistance of Rs 1,000 to girl students for pursuing higher education (undergraduate degree, diploma or ITI courses) through direct transfer to the bank account.

Eligibility: Girl students who studied from Class 6 to Class 12 in Tamil Nadu government or government-aided schools and who enrol in a recognised higher education institution. The student must be a resident of Tamil Nadu.

Documents required: Aadhaar card, school transfer or study certificate for Classes 6 to 12, institution admission proof, and bank account details.

Application process: Apply through the higher education institution or the designated state education portal during the admission period.
