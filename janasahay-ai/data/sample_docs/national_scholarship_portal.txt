National Scholarship Portal - Post Matric Scholarship for Students
Sample demo notes - always verify details on the official portal scholarships.gov.in.

Benefits: Financial assistance covering course fees and maintenance allowance for students studying after Class 10 in recognised institutions. Amounts differ by course and by scheme.

Eligibility: Students from eligible categories (for example SC, ST, OBC and minority communities) who are enrolled in a recognised post-matric course. Family income limits apply and vary by scheme; for the Post Matric Scholarship for SC students the annual family income ceiling is about Rs 2.5 lakh. Students must have passed the previous examination.

Documents required: Aadhaar card, income certificate, caste or community certificate, previous year marksheet, bank passbook, and a bonafide certificate from the institution.

Application process: Register and apply online on the National Scholarship Portal each academic year. The institution and the nodal officer verify the application before the scholarship is released to the student's bank account.
