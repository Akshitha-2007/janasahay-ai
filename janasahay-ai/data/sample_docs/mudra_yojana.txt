PM Mudra Yojana (PMMY)
Sample demo notes - always verify details on the official portal mudra.org.in.

Benefits: Collateral-free loans up to Rs 10 lakh for non-farm small and micro enterprises in manufacturing, trading and services. Loan categories: Shishu (up to Rs 50,000), Kishor (Rs 50,001 to Rs 5 lakh) and Tarun (Rs 5 lakh to Rs 10 lakh).

Eligibility: Any Indian citizen with a business plan for a non-farm income-generating activity can apply. There is no fixed income limit. Existing small businesses can apply for expansion.

Documents required: Identity proof (Aadhaar, voter ID or PAN), address proof, business proof or licence, quotations for machinery or goods to be purchased, passport size photographs, and bank statements where applicable.

Application process: Apply at any bank, regional rural bank, small finance bank, micro finance institution or NBFC, or online through the Udyamimitra portal.
